# Code of conduct

We're a community of people who help others for free. Let's act like it.

- Be respectful, whatever someone's experience, background or views.
- Talk about the code, not the person. Explain instead of putting people down.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- No insults, threats, harassment or discrimination.

If something goes wrong, tell the organization owners privately. They can remove comments,
commits or issues and block people who break these rules.
